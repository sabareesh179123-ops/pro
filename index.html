Frequently Asked Questions

How does a partnership differ from hiring a normal web developer?

Normal developers create a site and hand it over, leaving your school to deal with maintenance, bugs, updates, and hosting. Sabareesh acts as an ongoing digital department for your school—handling technical updates, security, content adjustments, and feature enhancements continuously.

Can school non-technical staff make routine updates?

Yes! We provide intuitive administrative portals so staff can easily upload notices or news. Alternatively, as part of our partnership, you can simply send us the content and our team will format and post it for you within hours.

How long does it take to deploy a new school portal?

A typical full school portal transformation takes between 3 to 6 weeks, including content migration, design approvals, administrative testing, and staff onboarding.
